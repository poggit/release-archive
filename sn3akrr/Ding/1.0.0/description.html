Use this plugin to play a sound when any projectile hits and damages another entity! Perfect for keeping track of when your shots land with bows, eggs, snowballs and much more!

How to configure

Once plugin starts, a default config will be created. Here you can modify the sound that plays on hit, along with the volume and pitch of the sound.

A list of sounds can be found at https://www.digminecraft.com/lists/sound_list_win10.php
