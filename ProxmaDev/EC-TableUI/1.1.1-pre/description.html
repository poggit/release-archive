EC-TableUI

An Enchantmenttable made with FormAPI.

How to use

  1. Own an enchantmenttable.
  2. Hold an item in hand.
  3. Tap the enchantmenttable with your item in hand.
  4. Select your enchantment.
  5. Enjoy!
