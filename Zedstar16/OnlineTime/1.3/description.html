OnlineTime

Measure how long players are spending on your server!

  • This current version is an important fix for mixed case player online times displaying as 0:00:00

Info:

  • Great for seeing player activity and finding the most active players on the server!
  • Use commands to view online times for session and total online times of yourself and other players
  • List of top active players
  • Online times will be measured as soon as plugin is running on server
  • Support for ScoreHud, get the Addon here

For support, help, and suggestions join the Support discord or create an issue here

Commands:

  • /ot total [player] (Get the total online time of yourself or another player)
  • /ot session [player] (Get the current session duration of yourself or another player)
  • /ot top [page] (Get the list of top most active players
  • /ot reset all (OPs only, reset all online time data)
  • /ot info (Displays version and credits)
