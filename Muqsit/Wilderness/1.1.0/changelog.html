Wilderness v1.0.0

  • Added world-selector config property. You can now configure whether you want to teleport the player in the world they're currently in, in a world picked at random from a list of worlds, or to a specific world.
  • Plugins can now register their own custom configurable wilderness behaviour by registering a behaviour/Behaviour.
  • Moved configurable options from config.yml to behaviour/wilderness-default.yml and removed lang.yml.

**NOTE: **You will need to delete your old configuration files.
