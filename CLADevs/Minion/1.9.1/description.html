This plugin adds 2 minions on your server the 2 minions are Farmer and Miner.

  • Farmer minion harvest the crops around the entity when its fully grown.
  • Miner minion will mine the block infront of the minion.

those blocks/crops mined/harvested will go into the chest behind you or the chest you linked.

How do i spawn the minion?

If you have permission you can type commands to get a nether star and tap on the ground.

  • /minion (type) - Give urself a minion.
  • /minion (type) (player) - gives minion to other players.
  • /minion (remove|rm|forceremove) - this allows to enter remove mode for minion.
