PiggyCustomEnchantsShop

PiggyCustomEnchantsShop is an add-on the the PiggyCustomEnchants plugin, adding shops.
You can either use SignShops or UIShops, which uses libFormAPI.

Supported Economy Plugins

  • EconomyAPI

Information

  • We do not support any spoons. Anything to do with spoons (Issues or PRs) will be ignored.
  • Check out our installation guide at PiggyDocs.
